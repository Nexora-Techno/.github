
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Nexora-Techno is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in our projects and communities.

We believe that collaboration, mutual respect, and constructive communication are essential to meaningful technological progress.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect.
- Communicating professionally and constructively.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Providing thoughtful and respectful feedback.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Trolling, abusive language, or disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats, stalking, or unwanted attention.
- Deliberately disrupting projects or community activities.
- Any other conduct considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Nexora-Techno organization spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Community communication channels.
- Official events and activities.
- Other spaces where individuals represent Nexora-Techno.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the organization maintainers.

Please include relevant information and evidence when possible.

Reports will be reviewed responsibly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

1. A private warning.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary restrictions.
4. Removal from community spaces.
5. Permanent exclusion from organization-managed spaces.

Maintainers may take appropriate action depending on the situation.

## Protection Against Retaliation

Retaliation against individuals who report violations or participate in investigations is unacceptable.
